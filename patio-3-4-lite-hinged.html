Where the door gets the most contact

Glass above. Solid lower panel below.

The lower panel is the part most likely to meet a chair, a pet, or everyday contact near the floor.

The 3/4 Lite Hinged door puts tempered, Low-E glass in the upper three-quarters of each panel and a solid fiberglass lower rail below it. Ball-bearing hinges support the swing, while compression weatherstripping presses against the frame when the panel closes.

Hinged movement

Glass in the upper three-quarters, a solid lower panel below.

Each panel is 250 lb fully glazed and hangs on ball-bearing hinges.

02 · The seal & the hardware

A seal that closes the same way every time

Compression weatherstripping presses against the frame when the door closes.

Compression weatherstripping closes against the frame under pressure rather than brushing across it. The core is CFC free polyurethane, and the frame is 100% composite waterproof material that will never rot.

No handle arrives fitted. Each panel is double bored at the factory for the handle and lockset you choose. The handle and lockset are not included.

Three glass styles

Choose clear, grilles, or divided light

All three styles keep the 3/4 lite layout and use tempered, Low-E glass. The difference is the look of the upper glass area.

Grilles between the glass

Grilles are sealed between the glass, so there is no grille surface to dust.

Simulated divided light

Simulated divided light adds surface-mounted bars for a more traditional profile.

Clear glass

Clear glass leaves the upper three-quarters visually open with no grille pattern.
