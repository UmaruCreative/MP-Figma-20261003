Frequently Asked Questions

About the 3/4 Lite Hinged Patio Door

Why is the lower panel solid?

Glass fills the upper three-quarters of each panel. The solid lower panel is the part most likely to meet a chair, a pet or everyday contact near the floor.

Does it come with a handle and lockset?

No. Each panel is double bored at the factory for the handle and lockset you choose; they are not included.

Which swing, handing and sizes are offered?

Inswing or outswing, left-hand or right-hand, in 60 × 80 in or 72 × 80 in, finished in White. Choose the swing and handing that match the opening before ordering.

What are its structural ratings?

PG40 for inswing and PG50 for outswing. Both panels swing on ball-bearing hinges in every configuration.

What does the warranty cover?

Frames, astragals and panels for life; the locking mechanism for 10 years; the factory painted finish for 2 years; tempered non-impact glass for 20 years, prorated. Coverage is for parts, not labour.
