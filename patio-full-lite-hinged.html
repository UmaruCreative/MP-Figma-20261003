See the four styles

Four glass styles

Choose the glass style and know what you will clean

Choose clear glass, simulated divided light, grilles between the glass, or blinds between the glass. The choice changes what you see and what you clean.

Grilles between the glass

The grid is sealed between the glass, so there are no grille bars on the surface to wipe.

Simulated divided light

Surface-mounted bars give the panel a traditional divided light profile. Clean the bar surfaces with the glass.

Clear glass

The glass spans edge to edge across the full lite, with no grille pattern to clean.

Blinds between the glass

A shade is sealed inside the glass cavity. No cords to manage. Nothing inside the cavity to dust.

In the crate

It arrives prehung in the selected configuration

The unit arrives in a 5.75 inch jamb with compression weatherstripping fitted. The selected glass style and finish are part of the unit. Both panels are glazed, hung on ball-bearing hinges, and double bored for hardware. Each fully glazed panel weighs 250 pounds.
