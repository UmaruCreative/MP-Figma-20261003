Frequently Asked Questions

About the Full Lite Hinged Patio Door

Does it come with a handle and lockset?

No. Each panel is double bored at the factory for the handle and lockset you choose, which are bought separately.

Which swing, handing and sizes are offered?

Inswing or outswing, left-hand or right-hand, in 60 × 80 in or 72 × 80 in. Confirm the swing and handing against your opening before ordering.

Which glass styles and finishes are available?

Clear glass, simulated divided light, grilles between the glass or blinds between the glass, finished in White or Black.

What are its performance ratings?

Inswing PG40 and outswing PG55 structurally, with a U-Factor of 0.29. Both panels swing on ball-bearing hinges and close against compression weatherstripping.

What does the warranty cover?

Frames, astragals and panels for life; the locking mechanism for 10 years; the factory painted finish for 2 years; non-impact glass for 20 years and glass with mini blinds for 5 years, both prorated. Coverage is for parts, not labour, and is void above 4,000 feet elevation.
