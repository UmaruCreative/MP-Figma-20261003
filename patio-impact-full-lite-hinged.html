Frequently Asked Questions

About the HVHZ Impact Full Lite Hinged Patio Door

Can I use it in a High Velocity Hurricane Zone?

It is engineered to resist high-wind events and carries a DP50 structural rating, tested with the impact glass in place. HVHZ approval must be confirmed against your local code before specifying.

Which swing, handing and size are offered?

Outswing only, left-hand or right-hand, in 72 × 80 in, finished in White.

Does it come with a handle and lockset?

No. Both panels are double bored at the factory, so the hardware you choose fits without further drilling; no handle or lockset arrives fitted.

What are its energy figures?

U-Factor 0.29 and SHGC 0.24 are recorded for this line, but no impact-specific NFRC rating is on file yet, so both are pending confirmation.

What does the warranty cover?

Frames, astragals and panels for life; the locking mechanism for 10 years; the factory painted finish for 2 years; the impact glass for 10 years, prorated. Coverage is for parts, not labour.
