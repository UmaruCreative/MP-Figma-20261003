URL NOTE
--------
The hierarchy above uses the production directory form (/why-composite/).
On disk the pages are flat files (why-composite.html) so the site opens and
browses correctly from the filesystem and from any static host. At deploy
time map each clean URL to its file, e.g.

    /why-composite/     ->  why-composite.html
    /real-projects/     ->  real-projects.html
    /blog/              ->  blog.html
    /warranty-support/  ->  warranty-support.html
    /entry-doors/       ->  entry-doors.html
    /patio-doors/       ->  patio-doors.html

Anchors are unchanged by the mapping. The <link rel="canonical"> on every page
already points at the clean URL form under https://www.mpdoors.com/.

7 · DESIGN SYSTEM
================================================================================

The interior pages were retoned so the whole site reads as one brand:

  Palette   Interior tokens now mirror the master homepage :root —
            paper #FDF6E6, ink #1C1608, muted #6E5A2C, gold #D2A63A on dark
            and #8A6100 on paper, with the dark bands warmed to #241B09.
            All text/background pairs clear WCAG AA at 6:1 or better.
  Type      Cormorant Garamond throughout, replacing EB Garamond, with the
            type scale lifted to suit its smaller x-height.
  Scrims    Hero and cinematic scrims warmed to the palette and strengthened
            so headlines hold over daylight photography at every viewport.
  New       Comparison table, filter chips, editorial list, long-form article
            and form primitives were added to home.css for the four new pages.

Behaviour is unchanged from the source: no build step, no framework, no CDN
scripts, reduced-motion respected, and the "Reduce animations" toggle works on
every page.
