Frequently Asked Questions

The questions that come up first

Where do I buy an MP Doors door?

In store and online at The Home Depot. No special order and no regional variant: the same certified door is sold in every climate zone. Configuration and lead time are confirmed at the point of purchase.

What is the frame actually made of?

A fiberglass panel on a 100% waterproof composite frame with full composite edging. It is engineered to resist rot, warp, split, delamination, denting and rust, rather than to be protected from them by a coating.

Is one door really certified for every climate zone?

Yes. ENERGY STAR certifies across four United States climate zones and MP Doors qualifies in all four with one composite door. Per-configuration NFRC figures are printed on each product page.

Can I use these in a hurricane zone?

The impact series carries Florida approval FL29714-R15 and is missile-rated to code. High-Velocity Hurricane Zone use should be confirmed against your local code before ordering.

Can I paint or stain the door?

It can be repainted, but a finish below LRV 57 may void coverage because a dark colour absorbs substantially more heat. Coverage is also void above 4,000 feet elevation.

How do I make a warranty claim?

Through the retailer of purchase, with the order details and photographs of the issue. Coverage is for parts rather than labour; the warranty section on each product page sets out what applies to each component.
