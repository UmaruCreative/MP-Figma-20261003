Measurement & installation

Measure twice, in three places, and take the smallest

A door is ordered to the rough opening, not to the slab currently hanging in it. These are the numbers to have in hand before you buy, and the checks to run when the unit arrives.

Rough opening widthMeasured top, middle and bottom · smallest governs
Rough opening heightMeasured left, centre and right, to sub-floor · smallest governs
Square checkBoth diagonals within 1/4 in of each other
Jamb depthInterior finished surface to exterior sheathing
HandingDetermined from outside, looking in · fixed at manufacture
SwingInswing or outswing, confirmed against clearance both sides
Delivery inspectionTransit damage raised within the retailer's five-day window

On delivery

Unwrap and inspect on the day it lands

  • Check the glass across both faces in raking light before anything else.
  • Check the frame faces, the sill and the bottom rail for transit knocks.
  • Confirm size, handing, swing, glass pattern and finish against the order.
  • Photograph anything questionable and raise it with the retailer inside the inspection window.

On installation

Shimmed square, or nothing else matters

  • Set the sill level and fully supported along its length.
  • Shim at every hinge point and at the strike, then re-check both diagonals.
  • Close the door on a sheet of paper at four points around the seal; it should grip evenly.
  • Flash and seal the exterior perimeter to local practice before trimming out.

Guidance is general. Installation must follow the printed instructions supplied with the unit and any requirement of local code; where the two differ, local code governs.

Care

There is no refinishing cycle

A composite frame has no substrate underneath that needs the finish to survive, so maintenance is cleaning rather than protection.

Wash

Faces and frame

Mild soap and water, rinsed. No abrasives, no solvents, no pressure washing.

Clear

Sill and track

Grit in a gliding track is what wears rollers and weatherstrip. Vacuum it out.

Check

Weatherstrip, seasonally

Close the door on a sheet of paper at four points. It should grip evenly all round.

Frequently asked

The questions that come up first

If the answer you need is not here, the contact form below reaches a person.

Where do I buy an MP Doors door?

In store and online at The Home Depot. No special order and no regional variant: the same certified door is sold in every climate zone. Configuration and lead time are confirmed at the point of purchase.

What is the frame actually made of?

A fiberglass panel on a 100% waterproof composite frame with full composite edging. It is engineered to resist rot, warp, split, delamination, denting and rust, rather than to be protected from them by a coating.

Is one door really certified for every climate zone?

Yes. ENERGY STAR certifies across four United States climate zones and MP Doors qualifies in all four with one composite door. Per-configuration NFRC figures are printed on each product page.

Can I use these in a hurricane zone?

The impact series carries Florida approval FL29714-R15 and is missile-rated to code. High-Velocity Hurricane Zone use should be confirmed against your local code before ordering.

Can I paint or stain the door?

It can be repainted, but a finish below LRV 57 may void coverage because a dark colour absorbs substantially more heat. Coverage is also void above 4,000 feet elevation.

How do I make a warranty claim?

Through the retailer of purchase, with the order details and photographs of the issue. Coverage is for parts rather than labour; the tier table above sets out what applies to each component.
