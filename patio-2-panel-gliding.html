Built around the load, seal, and frame.

The moving panel weighs 250 lb fully glazed. Stainless tandem rollers carry it. Compression weatherstripping closes against the frame.

01 · The carriage

Stainless tandem rollers carry 250 lb per panel

Each fully glazed panel weighs 250 lb and rides on stainless steel tandem rollers.

Each carriage uses two wheels instead of one. The load is distributed across the track rather than concentrated at a single wheel. The handle set and lockset are fitted at the factory.

The handle set and lockset arrive factory-fitted, so those components are already part of the ordered unit.

Rollers Stainless tandem Hardware Factory-fitted handle set and lockset

02 · The seal

Compression weatherstripping closes against the frame

The seal closes by compression, not by brushing past the frame.

Compression weatherstripping is squeezed against the frame when the door closes. It does not rely on a brush seal sliding past the frame.

Behind the seal is a CFC free polyurethane foam core. Around the unit is 100% composite waterproof material that will never rot and is built for wet and dry seasonal changes.

Seal Compression Core CFC free polyurethane

03 · The glass

The glass changes the published performance figures

Twenty of 24 configurations meet ENERGY STAR in all four US climate zones. The four blinds-between-glass configurations are not certified.

Clear, simulated divided light, and grilles-between-glass use the standard glass figures of 0.26 U-Factor and 0.18 SHGC and are certified in all four zones. The four blinds-between-glass configurations use 0.30 U-Factor and 0.35 SHGC and are not certified.

The figures are listed separately so the blinds-between-glass configuration is not hidden inside an average.

Standard glass 0.26 U · 0.18 SHGC Blinds glass 0.30 U · 0.35 SHGC
