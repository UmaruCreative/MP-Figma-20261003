Frequently Asked Questions

About the Full Lite Gliding Patio Door

How are the panels carried?

Each fully glazed panel weighs 250 lb and rides on stainless steel tandem rollers: two wheels per carriage instead of one, so the load is spread across the track.

Is every configuration ENERGY STAR certified?

Twenty of the 24 are, in all four US climate zones: clear, simulated divided light and grilles between the glass, at U-Factor 0.26 and SHGC 0.18. The four blinds-between-the-glass configurations (0.30 / 0.35) are not certified.

Which sizes, glass styles and finishes are offered?

Sizes 60 × 80 in and 72 × 80 in; clear glass, simulated divided light, grilles between the glass or blinds between the glass; left-hand or right-hand sliding; Smooth White, or a woodgrain interior with a Smooth White exterior.

What arrives in the crate?

Both panels, glazed and hung in the frame, with the white handle set and lockset factory-fitted, compression weatherstripping and the stainless tandem rollers installed. Brickmould, a screen and a pet door are not included.

What does the warranty cover?

Frames, astragals and panels for life; the locking mechanism for 10 years; the factory painted finish for 2 years; non-impact glass for 20 years and glass with mini blinds for 5 years, both prorated.
