The construction

A door built to stay beautiful and strong

Beauty and long-lasting performance, expertly combined.

The Craftsman is a high-performance fiberglass panel with full composite edging and a complete composite frame system, making it 100% waterproof and highly resistant to rotting, warping, splitting, delaminating, denting, and rusting.

Its low-maintenance design gives you peace of mind: it will stay as beautiful and strong as the day it was installed, for many years to come.
