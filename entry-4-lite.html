Frequently Asked Questions

About the 4 Lite Entry Door

Which glass options are available?

WaterWave and Frost, both Low-E. The door is ENERGY STAR qualified.

Which finishes can I choose?

White, Black or Smooth Latte. Smooth Latte is offered with Frosted glass only.

What size is the 4 Lite?

One size: 36 × 80 in (3068). The actual unit size is 37-3/8 × 81-9/16 in, with a 4-9/16 in net frame depth.

Which way does it open?

Left-hand or right-hand inswing.

What warranty does it carry?
